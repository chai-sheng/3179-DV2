02 / WHEN

Follow the season,
not a single timetable.

Australia’s seasons run opposite to those in the Northern Hemisphere. The main pulse in this sample falls in winter and spring, but species and places have different windows.

of 2016–2025 observations were recorded from June to October.

BEHIND THE STORY

Sources, methods & care.

Two independent datasets support this story. The first records community observations; the second identifies important habitats. They answer different questions and are not joined as if they measured the same thing.

DATASET A

Community whale observations

iNaturalist Research-grade Observations Marine Subset, published by the Marine Biological Association through OBIS. Version 2.0, archive release 1 September 2026; accessed 15 October 2026.

3,082 records in a geographic window around mainland Australia and Tasmania. Records run from 8 June 2016 to 19 July 2026; this is a snapshot, not a live feed.

Biologically Important Areas

How the data were prepared

Observation queries selected four whale species within 110°–156°E and 46°–10°S. This rectangle is not an Australian EEZ boundary and excludes external territories. Occurrence IDs were checked for uniqueness. Subspecies were rolled up to their parent species, with the exact scientific identification retained.

East = NSW, Queensland and Jervis Bay; West = Western Australia; South = South Australia, Victoria and Tasmania; North = Northern Territory. Groups use the supplied state, not a new coordinate-based assignment. No baseline records are assigned to North. Unmapped states remain Unassigned: 460 baseline records, 497 including 2026.

Research grade describes community-supported identification, not representative sampling. No correction for observer effort was possible. Eleven records have text suggesting remains, stranding or death and need review; this flag is not a validated live/dead classification. Accordingly, the site uses “observations” rather than claiming every record is a live sighting.

BIA geometry was simplified to 0.01° with four decimal places and clipped to an overview window. Interior ring grouping and winding were normalised for map rendering. No exact habitat areas, distances or legal boundaries are calculated. The seasonal map retains six selected source IDs and original seasonal text. Month filtering uses those explicit windows; it does not imply whale absence outside them.

Blue observations include the pygmy blue subspecies; the BIA category remains explicitly “Pygmy Blue Whale”. Blank seasonal notes were not treated as an absence of whale activity.
